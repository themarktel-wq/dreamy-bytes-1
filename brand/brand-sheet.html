Brand identity

Lahore Mobile

The mark is an L-frame: the L of Lahore holds a phone, the way a wholesaler's shelf holds stock for the shops around it. It is two simple strokes, so it stays sharp from a shop sign down to a 16-pixel browser tab.

The idea

Three things in one mark.

L for Lahore

A strong, rounded L: the city the business is built in, and the first letter of the name.

The phone

Panels, glass, covers and chargers: everything the shop sells starts with the phone.

Held together

The L supports the phone like a bracket holds stock. A dependable supplier behind every shop.

App icon and sizes

The icon on a shop owner's home screen, and the mark at small sizes.

Colours

Using the logo

Files are in the brand folder of the project.

Type. "LAHORE MOBILE" is set in Archivo ExtraBold. The line under it is IBM Plex Sans SemiBold with wide spacing, and the Urdu name is Noto Nastaliq Urdu.
Space. Keep clear space around the logo at least as wide as the L's stroke times three. Do not stretch it, rotate it, add shadows or put it on a busy photo.
On dark backgrounds use the white tile with the navy L (logo-horizontal-dark.svg). On white, use the navy tile.
Printing. For signboards, visiting cards and stickers, use the SVG files. They scale to any size without blur.
