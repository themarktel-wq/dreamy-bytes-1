**The gap we fill:** the market has expensive, long-format courses at one end and free but scattered YouTube advice at the other. Nobody owns **affordable, structured, Pakistan-specific toolkits** that you can act on the same day. That gap is Skillstack's position.

**Competitor visual audit:** most "online earning" brands and creators in Pakistan use dark backgrounds, neon green/gold, money and laptop stock photos, and income screenshots. Skillstack deliberately goes the other way (light ivory, violet, editorial type, native chat/notes formats) so it stands out in the feed and reads as trustworthy.

## 4. Product-hunting method we used (repeat it monthly)

1. **Demand signals** — Facebook groups (freelancing, Daraz sellers, home business), YouTube Pakistan search suggestions, TikTok comments, Google Trends (region: Pakistan).
2. **Repeated questions** — any question asked 10+ times in groups is a product ("Fiverr pe order kaise aaye?", "Daraz commission kitna hai?").
3. **Proof of payment** — similar products already selling on Gumroad/Etsy/Instagram/Daraz = buyers exist.
4. **Localise** — take a proven global product type (prompt pack, budget planner, CV templates) and make it Pakistan-specific.
5. **Price ladder** — entry (Rs 499–799), core (Rs 999–1,499), premium (Rs 1,999), bundle (Rs 4,999).

### Monthly product-hunting checklist
- [ ] Scan 5 Facebook groups & 20 TikTok comment sections — note top 10 repeated questions
- [ ] Check Google Trends Pakistan for "online earning", "ChatGPT", "Fiverr", "Daraz", "freelancing"
- [ ] Look at the 10 best-selling digital products on Gumroad/Etsy in our categories
- [ ] Check our own WhatsApp: what did customers ask for that we don't sell?
- [ ] Shortlist 3 ideas → pre-sell on Instagram story poll → build the winner

## 6. Pricing & offer strategy

- **Main money-maker = All-Access Pass (Rs 4,999).** Push it in every ad's retargeting stage and as cart upsell (already built into the website).
- **Entry products (Rs 499–799)** exist to get the first purchase cheaply in ads; then upsell bundle on WhatsApp after delivery.
- **"Compare-at" prices (strikethrough):** they represent the product's value. If you prefer to be extra safe legally, rename the label to "Value" instead of a "was" price (Pakistan consumer laws and Meta ad policies dislike fake discounts). Never invent a fake countdown.
- **Seasonal pushes:** 11.11, 12.12, Ramadan, Eid, 14 August, results season (students), new-year goals.
