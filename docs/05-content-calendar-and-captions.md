# 05 — Content Research, 9 Launch Posts & 30-Day Plan

> **Khulasa:** Research se pata chala ke Pakistan mein Reels sab se zyada reach dete hain, Roman Urdu comments aur engagement mein jeetta hai, humour/meme aur "asli" lagne wala content polished ads se behtar chalta hai, aur log creators/dosto ki recommendation par khareedte hain. Isi liye 9 posts mein har ek ka format alag hai: data hook, meme, WhatsApp chat, notes list, education, comparison, poll aur offer. Har post ka ek kaam hai (reach, save, comment ya sale).

## 2. Content pillars

| Pillar | Share | Goal | Formats |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Teach** (save-worthy) | 35% | Saves & shares → reach | Notes lists, carousels, "how to get paid" |
| **Relate** (meme / humour) | 25% | Shares & tags → new followers | Day 1 vs Day 90, salary memes, skits |
| **Prove** (data, comparisons, real reviews) | 20% | Trust | Stats with sources, course vs kit, buyer screenshots (with permission) |
| **Sell** (offers, product demos) | 20% | Orders | Product walkthrough Reels, offer posts, WhatsApp chat stories |

## 6. 30-day organic plan (after launch)
Post 5×/week + stories daily. Every Monday, review which pillar got the most **saves and shares** and make more of it.

| Week | Mon (Teach) | Tue (Relate) | Wed (Sell) | Thu (Prove) | Sat (Teach/Relate) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reel: 3 prompts for Fiverr gigs | Meme: client says "urgent hai" | Reel: inside the All-Access (scroll) | Course vs kit Reel | Carousel: gig title formula |
| 2 | Carousel: CV before/after | Skit: "Boost karte ho, sales nahi aati" | Chat story: WhatsApp objection | Real buyer screenshot (with permission) | Notes: 5 free AI tools |
| 3 | Reel: Daraz profit math | Meme: salary day vs 20 tareekh | Reel: Fiverr Blueprint walkthrough | Poll: Fiverr or Upwork results | Carousel: Payoneer setup steps |
| 4 | Notes: interview answers | Relatable: "freelancer ki raat 3 baje" | Offer reminder + FAQ | Founder story Reel | Monthly recap + what's next |

**Stories daily:** a poll or quiz, one tip, one product screenshot, a "how to order" reminder twice a week.
**Engagement:** reply to every comment within 1 hour on launch week (comments boost reach). DM keyword replies (FIVERR, PROMPTS, ALL) can be automated with ManyChat later.

## 7. Creator & affiliate plan (month 2+)
Research shows Pakistani creators can't earn from Instagram directly, so affiliate income appeals to them.
- Recruit 10 micro-creators (5k–50k followers) in freelancing, student and small-business niches.
- Offer 25–30% commission per sale with a personal discount code, plus a free All-Access pass.
- Brief them to make **honest** UGC: "I tried the Fiverr Blueprint, here's what I changed in my gig." No income claims.
- Use their best videos as ads (with written permission). See Phase 2 in `docs/04`.
