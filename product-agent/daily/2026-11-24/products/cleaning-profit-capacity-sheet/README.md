# Cleaning Business Profit & Capacity Sheet

A spreadsheet that shows what each recurring cleaning client really earns you. It calculates true cost per visit (labor with burden and overhead, paid drive time, mileage, supplies), profit per visit, margin, profit per labor hour and the price you would need to hit your target margin. A capacity sheet shows how many hours each cleaner has booked and how many more average clients fit.

## What is inside
- **Start Here** - short instructions.
- **Settings** - wage, payroll burden %, overhead per labor hour, cost per mile, supplies %, target margin, minimum margin, weeks per month, target utilisation, visits per month for each frequency.
- **Clients** - up to 50 recurring clients: price, hours on site, crew size, paid drive minutes, miles. Auto columns: cost per visit, profit, margin %, profit per labor hour, status (On target / Thin / Below floor / Losing money), price needed for target margin, increase needed %, monthly revenue and profit, extra revenue if repriced.
- **Capacity** - up to 8 cleaners: available vs booked hours per week, utilisation, overbooked warning, team totals and how many more average clients fit.
- **Dashboard** - monthly revenue, profit, margin, profit per labor hour, clients to reprice, extra revenue if repriced, utilisation and a status chart.
- Nine fictional sample clients so you can see it working (delete them and type your own).

## Who it is for
Growing cleaning business owners and team leads (about 3-8 cleaners) who suspect some recurring clients lose money and want a clear repricing list.

## Good to know
- Works in Excel 2016 or later, Excel for Mac, Excel Online, Google Sheets (upload, then Open with Google Sheets) and LibreOffice. No macros.
- Every starting number is an example, not an industry average. Replace them with your own costs.
- For crew jobs all crew labor hours are counted under the lead cleaner, so per-cleaner utilisation is a planning estimate.
- Planning tool only. It is not tax, accounting or financial advice.
