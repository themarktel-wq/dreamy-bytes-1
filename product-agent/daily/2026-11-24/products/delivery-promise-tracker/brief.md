# Brief: Delivery Promise Tracker for Photographers (d20261124-10)
- Niche: wedding/event photographers. Persona: part-time wedding photographer, 10-20 weddings/yr.
- Concept: backlog-vs-promise forecaster. CRMs (HoneyBook, Dubsado, Studio Ninja) track jobs, not editing capacity.
- Evidence (research-photographers.md): H2 (Shotkit survey: most deliver in 2-4 or 4-8 weeks, editing the main cause of delay), H3 (editing ~12-14 h/wedding, surveys), H4 (client complaints on turnaround), H5 (delivery dates in contracts). All Med/Low confidence; competitor-gap claim not exhaustively verified.
- Pitch: know which deliveries are at risk early, send honest updates, fewer late-gallery disputes.
- Price: USD 39 (plan). Suggested ad angle: "Your couples are asking where their photos are. Know before they do." No performance claims have been tested.
- Deviation from spec: forecast start also floors at today (a job cannot be edited in the past); documented in README. Built as hand-written single-file HTML (not tools/saas kit, which adds licence gating that the vault does not need).
