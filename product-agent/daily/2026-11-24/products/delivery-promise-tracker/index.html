Delivery Promise Tracker

Promised dates vs. your real editing queue. Data stays in this browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent anywhere.

Sample data is loaded so you can see how it works (sample couples and dates are made up). Use Clear all and add your own jobs.

1. Your editing capacity

Default 12 hours per wedding is an editable starting point: one survey of wedding photographers (Fstoppers) reported an average of about 12 editing hours per wedding, another industry source says 14. Your own number depends on image count, culling and style, so set it per job. This is not a promise about your workflow.

Forecast: jobs are edited in order of promised date. Each job starts on the later of its wedding date, today and the finish of the job before it, then takes edit hours / weekly capacity x 7 calendar days (rounded up). Green = on time. Amber = up to 7 days late. Red = more than 7 days late. It is an estimate, not a guarantee.

5. Client update message

Messages quote the forecast finish plus your buffer days, so you do not over-promise again. Read and edit before sending.
