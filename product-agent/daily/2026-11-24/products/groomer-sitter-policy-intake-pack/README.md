# Groomer & Sitter Policy and Intake Pack

One fill-in document set for pet groomers, dog walkers and pet sitters, so clients know the rules before the first visit. It covers the client and pet intake form, a grooming policy (deposit, late-cancel and no-show, matting consent, vaccination check, pick-up), a dog walking and pet sitting service agreement skeleton, an optional photo release and an incident note. Every document has bracketed [fields] for your own prices, deposit and rules; nothing is pre-decided for you.

**What you get**
- 00 Start Here guide
- 01 Client and Pet Intake Form (owner, pet, vet, vaccinations, health, behaviour disclosure)
- 02 Grooming Policy (deposit, cancellations and no-shows, late arrival, matting and shave consent, vaccinations, behaviour, pick-up)
- 03 Dog Walking and Pet Sitting Agreement (services, fees, packages, keys, emergencies, liability, ending)
- 04 Photo and Video Release (records only / marketing choices)
- 05 Incident Note
- Editable Word (.docx) files plus PDF copies

**Who it is for:** solo and small-team groomers, dog walkers and pet sitters who want clear signed policies without paying a lawyer to start from a blank page.

**Important:** templates only, not legal advice. Check the wording for your country or state before use.
