# GroomQuote: Matting-Proof Grooming Price Calculator

A single-file price calculator for dog groomers. Build your own size x service price grid, add coat-condition and matting tiers with surcharges, and settle the "surprise fee" conversation at drop-off with a printable check-in form that has a matting consent line, then hand over an itemised invoice at pickup. Open `index.html` in any browser. No install, no account, works offline.

## What you get
- Price grid editor: 4 size tiers x 3 services (rename and edit everything), plus base minutes for each.
- Coat tiers (normal, heavy shedding, matted light / moderate / severe) with your own surcharge and time multiplier.
- Add-ons (teeth, nail grind, de-shed, flea bath) with price and minutes; add your own.
- Live estimate: base price, coat surcharge, add-ons, tax, total, deposit, estimated time and number of slots, balance at pickup.
- Deposit rule: % of price kept between your minimum and maximum.
- Printable check-in form (estimate, coat tier, consent wording you can edit, signature lines) and printable itemised invoice. Print or save as PDF from the browser; or copy invoice text.
- Saved visits list, JSON backup and restore. Data stays in your browser.

## Who it is for
Solo and mobile groomers who argue about matting surcharges and price flat rates that ignore labor.

## Good to know
- Sample prices, minutes and surcharges are examples only. They are not market or breed prices; set your own.
- Consent wording is a template, not legal advice. Adapt it to your local rules.
- Tested in Chromium at desktop and 375 px phone width; not tested in Safari or Firefox. Use Export JSON before clearing browser data.
