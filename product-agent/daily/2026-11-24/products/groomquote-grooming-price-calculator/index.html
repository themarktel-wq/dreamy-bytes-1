How GroomQuote works

Price = base price for the dog's size and service + the coat-tier surcharge + add-ons. Time = base minutes x the coat-tier multiplier + add-on minutes, rounded up to your slot length.

Deposit = deposit % x price, kept between your minimum and maximum.

At check-in choose the coat tier with the owner and print the check-in form: it has a consent line naming the tier and the surcharge, so the matting price is agreed before the dog is groomed. Print the itemised invoice at pickup.

Honest notes. All prices, times and tier rules are your own settings; the examples are not market data. The consent wording is a template, not legal advice: adapt it to your local rules. Data is stored in this browser only: use Export JSON to back up.
