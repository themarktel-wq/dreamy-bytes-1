# PawReport: Client Visit Report Card Generator

A single HTML file that turns a 30-second form into a polished, branded visit report for your pet-care client. Tick what happened (pee, poop, water, meal, medication, play), pick a mood, add a note and up to three photos, then save the report as an image, copy it as a WhatsApp-ready message, or print it to PDF. Add your logo, colour and footer line once and every report looks like your business. Open it in any browser; no install, no account, and photos never leave your device.

**Features**
- Branded report card: business name, logo, accent colour, footer line
- Quick checklist toggles and a five-option mood picker
- Notes and three photo slots (photos are shrunk on your device)
- Save as PNG image, copy as text for WhatsApp or SMS, print or save as PDF
- Visit history on your device (text only, photos are not stored) with reopen and delete
- Backup and restore of your brand and history as a JSON file
- Works on phone and desktop, offline

**Who it is for:** independent dog walkers and pet sitters who want professional updates for clients without paying monthly for full sitter software.

**Notes:** data is stored in your browser; use Back up before clearing browser data. Emoji look depends on your device.

Price: Rs 7,000 PKR (about $25 USD)
