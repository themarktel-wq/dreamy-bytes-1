How QuoteSweep works

1. Fill in My costs & times once (labor, overhead, margin, how many minutes each room takes you).

2. For each job enter rooms, condition, pets and add-ons. QuoteSweep adds up labor minutes, multiplies by your loaded hourly cost, and divides by (1 - margin) to get the price that leaves your target margin.

3. Check recurring discounts: each row shows the new price and the margin you keep. Red means below your warning line.

4. Print the client estimate or copy it as text. The client never sees your costs or margin.

Honest notes. Minutes and multipliers are starter examples you must calibrate with a few timed real jobs. Not tax or accounting advice. Data is stored only in this browser (localStorage): use Export JSON to back up.
