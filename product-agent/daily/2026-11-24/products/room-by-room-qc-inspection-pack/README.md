# Room-by-Room QC Inspection Pack

Inspection scorecards for cleaning companies, built around the cancellation cause owners worry about most: inconsistent quality between crews. A supervisor can audit a home in about 10 minutes, and the client gets proof of what was done. Eight editable Word files, each with a print-ready PDF.

## What is inside
- **Start Here: how to score** - scoring method (1 / 0 / NA), pass-mark box and a worked example.
- **Standard Clean Scorecard** - 8 rooms, 6-10 checks each.
- **Deep Clean Scorecard** - 10 rooms, 6-10 checks each.
- **Move-Out Inspection Scorecard** - 10 rooms, 6-10 checks each.
- **Photo-Proof Sheet** - before / after photo slots per area.
- **Client Visit Summary slips** - "what we did today", two per page, with a rating line.
- **Crew Onboarding SOP and training checklist** - fill-in procedures and a sign-off table.
- **Cleaner-Swap Handover Note** - preferences, trouble spots and a sample client message.

Every file is .docx (edit in Word, Google Docs or LibreOffice) and .pdf (print as is).

## How scoring works
Write 1 if a check meets your standard, 0 if not, NA if it does not apply. Score % = 1s / (1s + 0s) x 100. You set your own pass mark. Example: 21 ones and 3 zeros = 87.5%.

## Who it is for
Cleaning business owners and team leads who want a consistent standard across crews and simple proof of work for clients.
