# Shot List & Day Timeline Builder

A single-file tool that builds a wedding-day schedule backwards from the ceremony start time and checks whether your family group shots actually fit. Change the ceremony time and the whole day, including travel buffers and the golden-hour portrait slot, recalculates. Print it for the couple or copy it as text.

## What you get
- Ceremony time, length and venue travel times in, a timed schedule out (photographer start time included)
- Pre-ceremony and post-ceremony blocks you can add, delete and rename; every duration is editable
- Automatic travel and arrival buffers
- Family group shot list (name, how many, minutes each) with a running total; the formals block length is calculated from it
- A warning when group shots exceed the limit you set
- Golden-hour portrait slot pinned to the sunset time you type in (no internet, no API) with a warning if earlier blocks make it impossible
- Print-ready sheets (timeline and shot list) via your browser's Print / Save as PDF
- Copy as text, JSON export/import so you and the couple can share the same plan
- Saves in your browser, works offline, phone friendly

## Good to know
All starting durations (for example 3 minutes per group shot, 60-minute cocktail hour) are editable examples, not industry standards. Look up sunset for the venue and date and type it in.

## Who it is for
Part-time and solo wedding photographers who build timelines by hand and lose time to rushed formals and unrealistic schedules.

## Not included
No online sharing portal, client login or sunset lookup. Data lives in your own browser; export JSON to back up or share.
