# Brief: Shot List & Day Timeline Builder (d20261124-13)
- Niche: wedding photographers. Persona: part-time wedding photographer.
- Concept: timeline generated backwards from the ceremony start (travel and buffers included) plus a group-shot list that proves the formals fit; static templates cannot recalculate.
- Evidence: H4 (client complaints include style/time issues, Med), H3 (photographers spend little of the day shooting, Med). Evidence is INDIRECT (Low) per plan; this is the lowest-confidence software item.
- Pitch: fewer rushed formals, an agreed schedule, client-ready printout. Nothing about outcomes has been tested with real photographers.
- Not claimed: no industry-standard durations; all defaults labelled as editable examples. Competitors (Pixieset, Aisle Planner, free Etsy timelines) were NOT verified in research.
- Built as hand-written single-file HTML, not the tools/saas kit (no licence gating needed for the vault).
