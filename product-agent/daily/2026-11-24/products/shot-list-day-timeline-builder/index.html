Shot List & Day Timeline Builder

Builds the wedding-day schedule backwards from the ceremony time, and checks that your family group shots actually fit. Data stays in this browser.

The values loaded are editable examples, not industry standards. Change every duration to match your own pace and the venue.

1. The day

Sunset is typed in by you (look it up for the venue and date); this tool makes no network requests.

2. Before the ceremony

In time order. Travel and the arrival buffer are added automatically right before the ceremony.

3. After the ceremony

The "Family formals" block length is calculated from your group shot list. The golden-hour slot is pinned to sunset.
