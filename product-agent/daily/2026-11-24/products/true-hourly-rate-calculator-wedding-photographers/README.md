# True Hourly Rate Calculator for Wedding Photographers

An Excel / Google Sheets workbook that adds the invisible hours (editing, client admin, travel) to every wedding and shows what you really earn per hour. It also tells you the package price you need to hit your target income, and whether outsourcing your editing would pay off.

## What you get
- Inputs sheet: weddings per year, package price, direct costs, yearly overheads, target income, and hours per wedding for shooting, editing, admin and travel
- True hourly rate per wedding, the flattering shoot-only rate for comparison, and the gap between them
- Annual revenue, annual profit and true hourly rate after overheads
- Package price needed for your target income, and how many weddings you would need at your current price
- Outsourced-editing scenario: editor cost per wedding in, new hourly rate, profit change, hours freed and the cost of each hour saved
- Edit-hours sensitivity table (6 to 20 hours) with a chart
- Sources and Notes sheet that says which default is survey-based and which are examples
- Works in Excel 2016+, Excel Online, Google Sheets and LibreOffice; no macros; any currency

## Good to know
Only the 12 editing hours per wedding default comes from a source (a Fstoppers survey average; another source says 14). Every other starting number is an example to replace with your own. Taxes are not modelled. This is a planning tool, not financial, tax or legal advice.

## Who it is for
Full-time and serious part-time wedding photographers who suspect they are underpricing because editing and admin hours are hidden.
