# Brief: True Hourly Rate Calculator for Wedding Photographers (d20261124-12)
- Niche: wedding photographers. Persona: full-time studio owner.
- Concept: true hourly rate including invisible hours + price needed for target income + outsourcing what-if. Generic wedding pricing sheets exist; this one is built around the editing-hours gap.
- Evidence: H3 (surveys: ~4% of work time shooting, ~12-14 h editing per wedding; Med, vendor figures Low), H8 (income spread, Low-Med; not used as defaults). Competitors listed in plan were not verified; the score is 24/40, the minimum gate.
- Pitch: "Know what you really earn per hour." No outcome claims. Defaults other than 12 h editing are illustrative and labelled so.
- Built with openpyxl directly (formulas live in the sheet), recalculated in LibreOffice and checked against an independent Python model.
