# WalkWise: Dog Walker Package & Profit Planner

A single HTML file that tells a dog walker or pet sitter what a walk, a prepaid package or a whole day of walks really earns per hour after drive time, fees and travel cost. Set your rate card, build a package with your own discount and see the largest discount that still reaches your target hourly figure. Compare a marketplace fee with direct payment fees using numbers you enter yourself, and print a clean client price sheet. Open it in any browser; no install, no account, nothing is uploaded.

**Features**
- Rate card: 15 / 30 / 45 / 60 minute walks, extra dog, puppy add-on, holiday surcharge %
- Package builder: walks, length, dogs, discount %, drive time and travel: package price, price per walk, net after costs, net per hour with and without discount, and the maximum discount that still meets your target
- Daily route sheet: one row per walk, live price, revenue, fees, travel cost, total time and net per hour
- Marketplace vs direct: enter the platform fee and your payment fee; see what you keep per walk and per month and the marketplace price needed to match direct
- Printable client price sheet (Print or save as PDF)
- Saves in your browser; export/import your data as a JSON backup
- Works on phone and desktop

**Who it is for:** independent dog walkers and pet sitters (1 to 3 staff) who price by gut feeling, discount ad hoc or want to see if a marketplace is worth its fee.

**Notes:** starting numbers are examples. The only market figure shown is a labelled reference (Rover's published national average of about $21.45 per 30-minute walk); no platform commission is assumed. Not tax or financial advice.
