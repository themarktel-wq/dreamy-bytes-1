# Wedding Photography Contract + Client Guide Pack

Four editable Word documents (with PDF copies) built around the points that most often cause disputes between couples and photographers: when the photos arrive, how long the gallery stays online, how many images to expect, and what editing style was agreed. Fill in the yellow [bracketed fields], delete the options you do not need, and send.

## What is inside
1. **Wedding Photography Agreement**: key-terms table plus 14 clauses with fields for delivery window and promised date, delay notice and remedy, gallery access months and expiry, image-count range, editing style and RAW files, copyright and portfolio use, retainer, cancellation, second photographer, travel, equipment failure and force majeure
2. **Client Guide** (client-facing): what happens before, on and after the wedding, delivery timeline, gallery access, image count and style, using your photos
3. **Client Questionnaire**: couple, venues and timing, people and family groups, style and priorities, delivery
4. **Delivery and Gallery Email Set**: delivery, "date at risk" heads-up, on-track update, late-delivery apology, and two gallery-expiry reminders

## Good to know
- Every field you must fill is highlighted yellow and written in [SQUARE BRACKETS]; search for "[" to find any you missed.
- This is a template only, not legal advice. Contract rules differ by country and state. Have a lawyer review it for your jurisdiction before you use it. The contract footer says so.
- The text does not state industry-standard delivery times or image counts; you choose the numbers.

## Who it is for
Part-time and full-time wedding photographers who want clear written promises about turnaround, gallery access and image count.
