## Personas
1. **Solo/2-person maid owner (US/UK/CA):** 5-25 recurring clients, quotes by gut feel on the phone, uses notes app/WhatsApp. Trigger: a job that ate 6 hours for a 3-hour price. Objection: "monthly software fees per cleaner".
2. **Growing team lead (3-8 cleaners):** pays ZenMaid/Jobber, wants margin visibility and fewer cancellations; trigger: losing a long-term client after a missed spot.

## Pain points (paraphrased, Low-Med confidence)
Underquoting; per-user + per-SMS fees; clients cancelling after inconsistent quality; no systematic first-90-day follow-up; checklists on paper.
