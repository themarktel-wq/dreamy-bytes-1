## Personas
1. **Solo/mobile groomer:** 15-30 dogs/week, books by text, loses slots to no-shows, argues about matting at pickup. Trigger: an empty 2-hour slot after a no-call.
2. **Dog walker / pet sitter (1-3 staff):** charges $20-30 per walk, discounts ad hoc, no idea of profit per hour after drive time. Trigger: clients asking for package deals or holiday rates.
