## Gaps (honest)
- r/WeddingPhotography threads were not returned; no Meta Ad Library; the Shotkit cost-analysis numbers (weddings/year, price) were not retrievable; HoneyBook price facts conflict across sources (use as a "price anxiety" signal only).
- Gate: sources >=3 independent (IBISWorld, Shotkit/Fstoppers/Petapixel surveys, WeddingWire, Capterra); quantitative yes (H2, H3); gap = existing CRMs (HoneyBook/Dubsado/Studio Ninja) are subscription generalists; none found that tracks post-wedding delivery promises vs editing workload as a standalone cheap tool (not verified exhaustively; Med-Low). Only 3 clean competitor prices for CRMs (HoneyBook, Dubsado, Studio Ninja) + Bloom: the "5 prices" check is only partly met, so photographer prices are set conservatively and flagged.

## Personas
1. **Part-time wedding photographer (10-20 weddings/yr):** edits nights/weekends, promises 4-8 weeks, backlog grows in peak season. Trigger: a couple emails asking where the gallery is.
2. **Full-time studio owner (25-40 weddings):** pays a CRM; wants to know true hourly rate after editing/admin hours; trigger: a price-hike email from their CRM.
