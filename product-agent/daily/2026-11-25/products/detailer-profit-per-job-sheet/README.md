# Detailer Profit-Per-Job Sheet

A spreadsheet that shows what each detailing package really earns you per hour. Enter your hourly pay target, cost per mile and payment fees once, list your packages, and log jobs. It calculates true cost per job (products, mileage, payment fees, job and drive time at your pay rate), profit and margin, hourly earnings before and after paying yourself, flags packages that lose money, and shows the price needed to reach your target margin, with a monthly summary.

## What is inside
- **Start Here** - short instructions.
- **Settings** - hourly pay target, vehicle cost per mile, payment fee % and fixed fee, target and minimum margin, year for the summary.
- **Packages** - up to 10 packages with typical price, hours, drive minutes, miles and product cost. Auto columns: cost, profit, margin, earnings per hour before wage, profit per hour after wage, status (On target / Thin / Below floor / Losing money), price needed for target margin, increase needed, jobs logged and average actual profit.
- **Jobs** - up to 200 jobs with the same calculations on the real numbers, plus a package dropdown and status colours.
- **Summary** - revenue, profit, margin, jobs, hourly earnings, jobs that lost money, best package by profit per hour, a month-by-month table with a chart, and package performance.
- 30 fictional sample jobs so you can see it working (delete them and enter your own).

## Who it is for
Solo and small-shop detailers (mobile or fixed location) who do not know which packages pay once travel and products are counted.

## Good to know
- Works in Excel 2016 or later, Excel for Mac, Excel Online, Google Sheets (upload, then Open with Google Sheets) and LibreOffice. No macros.
- All starting numbers are examples, not market prices or averages. Replace them with your own.
- Price needed = (products + mileage + fixed fee + your pay for the hours) / (1 - fee % - target margin).
- Planning tool only. It is not tax, accounting or financial advice.
