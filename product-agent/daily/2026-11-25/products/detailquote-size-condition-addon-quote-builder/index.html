How DetailQuote works

Price = package price for the vehicle size x (1 + condition surcharge) + add-ons + travel fee, plus tax. Deposit = deposit % x total.

Inspection items (paint correction, ceramic coating, anything you only price after seeing the paint) are never added to the total. They are printed as "quoted after inspection" with the range you type, so you do not promise a fixed price you cannot keep.

Print the quote or copy the text to send by message. The deposit amount and your cancellation notice are printed on it.
