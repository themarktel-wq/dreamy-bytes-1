# MoveOut Ledger: Itemized Deposit Statement Builder

A single HTML file that helps small landlords turn a move-out into a clear, itemized deposit statement. List each room item, record move-in and move-out condition, add the repair cost, receipt reference and photo reference, then choose to charge nothing (normal wear), the full cost, or a prorated amount based on age and useful life that you enter yourself. The tool totals deductions, shows the refund due (or amount owed), warns about charged items with no receipt or photo, counts down to your statement deadline, and prints a clean statement. Open it in any browser; no install, no account, nothing is uploaded.

**Features**
- Room-by-room items: move-in vs move-out condition, notes, photo reference, cost, receipt reference
- Charge methods: none, full cost, or optional proration = cost x (1 - age / useful life)
- Deposit, deductions and refund due (or amount owed) calculation
- Warnings: no receipt, no photo, same condition at move-in and move-out, prorate without useful life
- Deadline reminder: you enter the number of days your state or city requires; the tool counts from the move-out date
- Printable itemized statement and copy-as-text; save as PDF from the print dialog
- Saved in your browser; export/import JSON backup; works on phone and desktop

**Who it is for:** accidental and small landlords (1 to 5 units) who want consistent itemization at move-out.

**Important:** template tool, not legal advice. It does not know your state's rules on deductions, receipts or deadlines; you decide and enter them. Starting values are examples.
