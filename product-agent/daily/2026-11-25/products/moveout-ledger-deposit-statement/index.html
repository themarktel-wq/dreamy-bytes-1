MoveOut Ledger

Itemize deposit deductions room by room and print a clear statement. Works offline; nothing is uploaded.

Template tool, not legal advice. Rules on what you may deduct, itemization, receipts and deadlines differ by state and city. This tool does not know them: you enter the deadline days and decide what to charge. Starting values are examples.

Tenancy details

Deadline = move-out date + the number of days you enter. Look the rule up for where you operate; this tool does not assume one.

Room-by-room items

One row per room item or charge (for example "Kitchen: oven" or "Unpaid rent"). Compare move-in and move-out condition, then choose how to charge. "Photo ref" is a file name or note: photos are not uploaded.
