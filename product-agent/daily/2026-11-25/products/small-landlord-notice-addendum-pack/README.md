# Small Landlord Notice + Addendum Pack

A set of fill-in Word and PDF templates for small landlords with one to five units, so everyday messages follow one consistent process instead of ad hoc texts. It includes a late-rent reminder with a formal-notice template, a maintenance request procedure addendum, a move-in / move-out condition form, a move-out instructions letter, a deposit itemization cover letter and a rent increase notice. Rules differ by state and city, so every document has bracketed fields where you enter your own local notice periods, fees and required wording; nothing is pre-decided for you.

**What you get**
- 00 Start Here guide
- 01 Late Rent Reminder and Notice
- 02 Maintenance Request Procedure Addendum
- 03 Move-In / Move-Out Condition Form (rooms and appliances, photo reference column)
- 04 Move-Out Instructions Letter
- 05 Deposit Itemization Cover Letter
- 06 Rent Increase Notice
- Editable .docx files plus PDF copies

**Who it is for:** accidental and small landlords who want clear, consistent paperwork.

**Important:** templates only, not legal advice. Landlord-tenant laws vary by state, county and city; check your local rules or ask a qualified professional before use.
