# Tutor Income and Package Pricing Sheet

A spreadsheet for private tutors that shows what you really earn per hour once unpaid prep, admin, travel, cancellations, fees and software are counted. Enter your own rate, lessons per week, prep and admin time, fee percentage and cancellation assumptions, and the sheet gives your true hourly rate, compares pay-per-lesson with a prepaid package at your chosen discount, and works out how many lessons and students you need to reach an income target. A sensitivity tab shows what a higher rate, more lessons or more students would do.

**What you get**
- Calculator tab: 16 yellow inputs, results for pay-per-lesson, prepaid package and target
- True hourly rate and true rate as % of your advertised price
- Package discount impact on hourly rate and yearly income (using YOUR cancellation assumptions)
- Lessons per week and students needed for your target income
- Sensitivity tab: price change, lessons per week and number of students
- Start Here guide; works in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice; no macros

**Who it is for:** solo private tutors who want to price lessons and packages with confidence.

**Notes:** example numbers are placeholders, not market data. Fees and costs are entered by you: no platform commission is assumed. Not tax, legal or financial advice.
