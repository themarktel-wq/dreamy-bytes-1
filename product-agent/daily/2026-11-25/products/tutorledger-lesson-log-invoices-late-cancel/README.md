# TutorLedger: Lesson Log, Invoices and Late-Cancel Fees (Any Payment Method)

A payment-neutral ledger for independent tutors who bill families directly. Log lessons, let the app apply your own written late-cancel and no-show rule, track prepaid packages, record payments you receive by Zelle, Venmo, cash, bank transfer or check, and print a clear monthly statement for each family. No processing fee is built in because the tool never touches the money. Open `index.html` in any browser. No install, no account, works offline.

## What you get
- Families with students and an hourly rate each.
- Lesson log: date, minutes, outcome (done, cancelled by family with notice hours, no-show). The charge is worked out for you.
- Your rule: notice required (hours), late-cancel fee (% of lesson price), no-show fee (% of lesson price).
- Prepaid packages: price is charged to the account, lessons remaining are tracked, "use package" lessons cost nothing extra.
- Payments received with method and note; balance = charges - payments.
- Dashboard of who owes what; monthly statement per family (opening balance, lessons and fees, payments, balance due, package lessons left, how-to-pay text, cancellation policy), printable or saved as PDF from the browser, or copied as text.
- Ledger CSV export, JSON backup and restore. Data stays in your browser.
- Fictional sample families so you can see it working; remove them with one click.

## Who it is for
Solo tutors (maths, languages, test prep, music and more) who invoice families directly, wait for payments and feel awkward about cancellation fees.

## Good to know
- Percentages, notice window and policy text are yours to set. Charging late-cancel fees may be restricted in some places or by platform rules: check where you work. Wording is a template, not legal advice.
- If you do not enter the notice given for a cancellation, the app assumes zero hours (fee applies); the form asks you to enter it.
- Tested in Chromium at desktop and 375 px phone width; not tested in Safari or Firefox. Use Backup JSON or Export CSV regularly.
- Record-keeping tool only. It does not take payments, send emails, or give tax or accounting advice.
