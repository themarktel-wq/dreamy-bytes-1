How TutorLedger works

Balance = everything charged to the family minus the payments you recorded. Charges are: lessons done (rate x minutes / 60) unless paid from a package, late-cancel and no-show fees, and package purchases.

Late-cancel rule. If a family cancels with less notice than your threshold (hours), the fee is your fee % x the lesson price. Enough notice costs nothing. A no-show costs your no-show %. If you do not enter the notice given, the app assumes zero hours.

Packages. Lessons remaining = lessons bought - lessons marked "use package".

Payments are just records of money you received by Zelle, Venmo, cash, bank transfer or check, so no payment-processing fee is added by this tool.

Statements show the opening balance, that month's lessons and fees, payments, the closing balance and package lessons remaining, ready to print.

Honest notes. Sample families are fictional. Data is stored in this browser only: use Backup JSON or Export ledger CSV regularly. This is a record-keeping tool, not tax or accounting advice.
