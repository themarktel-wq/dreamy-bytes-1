## Gaps (honest)
- No Reddit/forum threads, no no-show rate figure for detailing, no Meta Ad Library, no detailing-only market size. Shopmonkey price not found. Evidence is the weakest of the three niches; passes the gate only on Medium-Low confidence (3+ independent source types: IBISWorld, directories, ~10 shop policy pages; quantitative signal: D1, D2, D4; gap: detailing apps start at $25-129/mo, and the quote/deposit/condition-report workflow is handled by templates scattered across shop sites).
- Competitor prices >=5: DetailProPOS, Urable, OrbisX, RoadFS, ManageMart (directories).

## Personas
1. **Solo mobile detailer:** books by DM/phone, no deposit rules, drives to jobs with no water/power. Trigger: wasted trip.
2. **Small shop owner (2-4 staff):** sells add-ons and coatings, needs consistent quotes and condition proof.
