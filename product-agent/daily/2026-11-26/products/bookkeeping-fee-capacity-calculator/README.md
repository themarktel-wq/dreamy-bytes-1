# Bookkeeping Fee and Capacity Calculator

An Excel / Google Sheets workbook for freelance bookkeepers and small practices. It shows what each client really pays you per hour once the time you spend chasing documents and on admin is counted, flags clients below your target rate, suggests the monthly fee that would reach your target, and shows how full your month is and how many more clients of average size would fit.

## What you get
- Settings: your target hourly rate, minimum acceptable rate, billable hours per month, practice-software cost per seat and number of seats (you enter your own price)
- Clients sheet (up to 20 clients): monthly fee, bookkeeping hours, chasing and admin hours, other direct cost
- Per client: effective hourly rate before and after software, software allocation, chasing and admin share of time, fee needed for your target rate, increase needed (amount and %), and a colour-coded status (OK / Below target / Below minimum)
- Capacity sheet: total fees and hours, utilisation, hours still free, how many average clients fit, portfolio hourly rate before and after software, software as a % of fees, value of chasing and admin time at your target rate, fee increases needed, and a "what if I dropped the Below-minimum clients" view
- Chart of effective hourly rate by client; Sources and Notes sheet
- Works in Excel 2016+, Excel Online, Google Sheets and LibreOffice; no macros; any currency

## Good to know
All sample clients, fees, hours and rates are made-up examples, not benchmarks. Replace them with your tracked hours; tracking chasing and admin time for a month is where most of the value comes from. Not modelled: tax, one-off project work, seasonality. This is a planning tool, not tax, legal or financial advice.

## Who it is for
Freelance bookkeepers and small practices who suspect some clients cost more time than they pay for.
