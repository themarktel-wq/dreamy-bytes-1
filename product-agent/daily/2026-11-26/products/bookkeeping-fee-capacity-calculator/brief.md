# Brief: Bookkeeping Fee and Capacity Calculator (d20261126-12)
- Niche: bookkeepers. Persona: freelance bookkeeper / small practice.
- Concept: effective hourly rate per client including chasing and admin time, fee needed for target rate, capacity view and drop-low-yield scenario.
- Evidence: B2 (Wolters Kluwer survey: 45% ranked chasing client information the 3rd most common firm issue; Med), B5 (practice software prices; vendor-written comparisons conflict, Med-Low), B3 (month-end close done in spreadsheets; vendor survey). Software price is entered by the user because published prices conflict.
- Pitch: "Reprice or drop low-yield clients; plan capacity." No outcome claims. All sample data is invented and labelled.
