Class Overbook Planner

How many bookings should you accept? Uses your own no-show rate to show the chance of turning people away and of empty spots. Runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

The starting numbers are examples. Enter your own capacity, no-show rate (from your attendance records), price and cost of turning someone away.

1. Your class

Assumption: every booked person shows up or not independently, with the same chance. Real classes can differ (friends booking together, bad weather, class type), so use this as a guide and check it against your own attendance over time.

2. Result for bookings

Bars = chance of each number of attendees. Dark purple = exactly at capacity, red = over capacity (someone must be turned away).

3. Compare overbooking levels

BookingsChance over capacityExpected turned awayExpected empty spotsExpected attendees servedExpected net

Net = revenue per attendee x expected attendees served - cost per turned-away person x expected turned away. It ignores everything else (goodwill, waitlists, retention). "Best net" is the best row by this simple measure, not a recommendation.

Edit the text. Replace [CANCELLATION WINDOW]. Check any consumer rules about refunds and credits where you operate.
