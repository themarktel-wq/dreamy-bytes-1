# DocChase: Client Document Request Tracker

A single-file tracker for freelance bookkeepers who spend their month chasing clients for bank statements, receipts and payroll reports. See at a glance what each client still owes, what is overdue and by how many days, and get ready-to-send reminder wording. Open `index.html` in any browser. No install, no account, no per-seat fees, works offline.

## What you get
- Clients, each with a recurring document checklist and a due day of the month (for example bank statement on the 5th, receipts on the 15th). One-click "standard checklist" you can edit.
- A monthly request cycle: every document starts as not requested; mark it requested or received. A document is overdue when today is past its due date and it has not been received. Statuses are kept per month, so each new month starts fresh.
- Month-end close readiness: documents received / required, per client and overall, with a progress bar.
- Overdue ranking by days overdue, and one reminder per client listing every overdue document, in a gentle, firm or final tone based on day thresholds you set. Copy and send it yourself.
- "Mark all not-yet-requested as requested today" for the start of the month.
- Checklist CSV import and export, status CSV export for any month, JSON backup and restore. Data stays in your browser.
- Fictional sample clients so you can see it working; remove them with one click.

## Who it is for
Freelance bookkeepers (about 5-25 clients) who lose time chasing clients for documents and do not want per-seat software fees.

## Good to know
- DocChase is a tracker: it does not email clients or connect to their files. You send the reminders yourself.
- The tone thresholds and the standard document list are examples you control.
- Tested in Chromium at desktop and 375 px phone width; not tested in Safari or Firefox. Use Backup JSON or the CSV exports regularly.
- Record-keeping tool only: not accounting, tax or legal advice.
