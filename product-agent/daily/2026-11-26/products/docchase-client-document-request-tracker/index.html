How DocChase works

1. Add each client and their recurring checklist (bank statement, card statement, receipts, payroll report...) with the day of the month each is due.

2. On "This month" every document starts as not requested. Mark them requested when you send your request and received when the file arrives. A document is overdue when today is after its due date and it is not received.

3. Month-end readiness = documents received / documents required, per client and overall.

4. "Overdue & reminders" ranks overdue documents and writes one reminder per client (gentle, firm or final by days overdue) that you copy and send yourself.

Honest notes. Sample clients are fictional. DocChase does not email or connect to client files: it is a tracker. Data is stored in this browser only: use Backup JSON or the CSV exports regularly.
