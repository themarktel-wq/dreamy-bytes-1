# Labor Cost vs Sales Planner

A weekly planning spreadsheet for cafe and restaurant managers. Type the sales forecast and the planned shift hours by role for each day, and see labor cost, labor % of sales, a flag when a day is over your own target, sales per labor hour, the sales needed to hit your target, and what a wage increase would do to your labor %.

## What is inside
- **Start Here** - short instructions.
- **Settings** - payroll burden %, your labor % target, a wage increase what-if %, and up to 6 roles with hourly rates.
- **Week plan** - Monday to Sunday: forecast sales, hours by role, total hours, base wages, payroll burden, labor cost (current wages and with the what-if), labor % (both), over-target flag, sales per labor hour, sales needed to hit target and the gap. Week totals in the last column.
- **Wage what-if table** - labor cost, labor % and extra cost per week and per year for raises of 0%, 3%, 5%, 8%, 10% and 15% (change the percentages to your own).
- Sample week with fictional numbers so you can see it working; replace the yellow cells with your own.

## Who it is for
Cafe and restaurant managers and owner-operators whose schedule is not tied to sales and who are watching wage costs rise.

## Good to know
- Works in Excel 2016 or later, Excel for Mac, Excel Online, Google Sheets (upload, then Open with Google Sheets) and LibreOffice. No macros. Copy the Week tab to plan more weeks.
- All rates, burden %, targets, sales and hours are examples, not industry benchmarks. Your labor % target is yours to set.
- Labor cost = sum of (hours x rate) x (1 + burden). Planning tool only: not payroll, tax or legal advice (overtime, breaks and local labor rules are not modelled).
