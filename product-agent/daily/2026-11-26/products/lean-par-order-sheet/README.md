# Lean Par and Order Sheet

A spreadsheet for cafe and food-truck owners who want to order leaner. Enter your weekly sales forecast per menu item and the recipe usage per portion, and the sheet works out weekly ingredient usage, par levels and how many whole supplier packs to order after subtracting what you have on hand. Cover days are capped by each item's shelf life, a safety percentage protects against stock-outs, and a supplier summary shows what each order will cost. A waste log shows what throwing food away costs you per item.

**What you get**
- Summary tab: cover days and safety % settings, total order cost, items to order, order cost by supplier
- Items tab: up to 25 ingredients with pack size, unit cost, shelf life, on-hand, par level, order quantity, order cost, shelf-life flag, waste cost
- Forecast tab: units per day for up to 15 menu items
- Recipes tab: usage per portion matrix
- Waste Log tab: 200 rows with item list and automatic cost
- Start Here guide; works in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice; no macros

**Who it is for:** cafe, bakery and food-truck owners and managers who order weekly and want fewer stock-outs and less waste.

**Notes:** the starting data is an example cafe, not real prices or sales. Forecasts are your own estimates; the sheet does not predict sales. Order = whole packs covering forecast usage plus safety stock, minus on-hand.
