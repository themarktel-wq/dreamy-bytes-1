# Month-End Close Tracker

An Excel / Google Sheets workbook for freelance bookkeepers and small practices that close several clients every month. Each client is a row, each close task is a column and every cell has a status, so you can see at a glance what is done, what is blocked, what is overdue and how much work is left each week.

## What you get
- Settings: month-end date being closed, today's date (type =TODAY() to follow the calendar), and five editable close tasks with a due day (days after month end) and estimated hours per client
- Clients sheet (up to 15): cadence, monthly fee, contact, assignee, and "Active this close" (Yes / No)
- Close Grid: status drop-down in every cell (Not started, In progress, Blocked, Done), colour-coded; tasks done, progress %, blocked tasks, overdue tasks and hours remaining per client; due dates calculated from the month end
- Summary: active clients, overall progress, clients fully closed, clients with blocked tasks, blocked and overdue totals, hours remaining
- Workload by week (hours left on open tasks, grouped by the week their due date falls in) and by assignee (hours, overdue, blocked)
- Chart of progress by client; Sources and Notes sheet
- Works in Excel 2016+, Excel Online, Google Sheets and LibreOffice; no macros

## How to use it each month
Set the new month-end date, reset all statuses to Not started, mark which clients are active this close, and work down the grid.

## Good to know
The sample clients, task names, due offsets and hours are made-up examples; replace them with your own checklist and estimates (they are not an industry standard). The workbook tracks work; it does not connect to accounting software. Not tax, legal or financial advice.

## Who it is for
Freelance bookkeepers and small practices that currently run the close from spreadsheets, sticky notes and email.
