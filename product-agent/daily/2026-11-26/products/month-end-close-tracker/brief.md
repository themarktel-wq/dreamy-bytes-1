# Brief: Month-End Close Tracker (d20261126-10)
- Niche: bookkeepers. Persona: freelance bookkeeper / small practice.
- Concept: client x task grid with statuses, due dates, blocked/overdue flags, progress and workload by week and person. Practice-management software (Financial Cents, TaxDome, Karbon) covers this at per-user monthly prices; this is a one-time spreadsheet.
- Evidence: B3 (Financial Cents 2026 report: more than half of firms run month-end close with spreadsheets, sticky notes or email; vendor, Med-Low) and B6 (old 2016 survey, Low; not used). 
- Pitch: "One grid for all clients; see blockers; weekly workload." No outcome claims. Sample data is invented and labelled.
