# PlateCost: Menu Item Cost and Price Calculator

A single-file plate-costing tool for food trucks, cafes and small restaurants. It costs every menu item from your ingredients, adds yield loss, waste, packaging and overhead, and shows the price you need for your target margin, not only a food-cost percentage. Open `index.html` in any browser. No install, no account, no monthly fee, works offline.

## What you get
- Ingredient library: unit, pack price, pack quantity and yield % (trim or cooking loss). Cost per unit is worked out for you.
- Recipe builder: ingredient quantities for the whole batch, plates per batch, packaging per plate, waste %, overhead per plate (or a default).
- Price needed = plate cost / (1 - target margin), the food-cost % at that price, and your real margin at your current menu price (flagged when below target).
- Supplier what-if: see every menu item re-costed if prices rise or fall by x%, then apply it permanently with one button.
- Printable cost sheet per item, CSV export of the whole menu, JSON backup and restore. Data stays in your browser.
- Fictional sample ingredients and two sample menu items; remove them with one click.

## Who it is for
Food truck, cafe and small restaurant owners who price by gut feel and get hit when ingredient prices jump.

## Good to know
- Sample ingredients, prices and quantities are fictional examples. The 30% food-cost figure is only an editable example: no industry benchmark is assumed.
- Use the same unit in recipes as in the pack (for example grams). Overhead per plate is whatever you allocate; enter your own figure.
- Tested in Chromium at desktop and 375 px phone width; not tested in Safari or Firefox. Back up with Backup JSON before clearing browser data.
- Costing tool only: not accounting, tax or nutrition advice.
