# Brief: PlateCost (d20261126-01)
- Niche: restaurants / food trucks. Type: html tool (software). Price USD 35 / PKR 9,800 (1 USD = 280 PKR).
- Unique concept: plate costing that includes packaging, waste/trim yield and overhead, and outputs the price needed for a target margin (not just a food-cost %), with a supplier-price what-if; costing software costs $15-250/mo (evidence R4, R5, R6 in research-restaurants-food-trucks.md).
- Persona: food truck / cafe owner. Pain: prices set by gut, ingredient cost jumps.
- Build: single-file index.html, pure JS, localStorage, CSV/JSON export.
- Deviations from plan: none material; added a per-recipe overhead override, a margin-vs-target flag and an "apply what-if" button.
