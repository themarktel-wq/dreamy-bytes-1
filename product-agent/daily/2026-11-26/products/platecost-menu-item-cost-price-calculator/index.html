Ingredient library

Cost per unit = pack price / (pack quantity x yield). Yield is the share you can actually use after trim or cooking loss (100% = no loss). Use the same unit in recipes as in the pack (for example grams).

Targets

Margin = (price - plate cost) / price. The food-cost % target is only an example you can change: it is not an industry rule. Overhead per plate is whatever you allocate (rent, labor, fuel, fees): enter your own figure.

Supplier price what-if

The what-if is shown live on the Menu items tab (columns marked "what-if") without changing your data. "Apply" multiplies every pack price by the percentage once, then resets the box to 0.

How PlateCost works

1. Add your ingredients with pack price, pack quantity and yield. 2. Build each menu item from ingredients (quantity for the whole batch), say how many plates the batch makes, and add packaging, waste % and overhead per plate. 3. Read the price needed for your target margin and the food-cost % at that price. Enter your current menu price to see your real margin.

Formulas. Ingredient cost per unit = pack price / (pack quantity x yield). Ingredient cost per plate = batch ingredient cost / plates x (1 + waste %). Plate cost = ingredient cost per plate + packaging + overhead. Price needed = plate cost / (1 - target margin). Food-cost % = ingredient cost per plate / price.

Honest notes. Sample ingredients, prices and quantities are fictional examples. No industry benchmark is assumed: the food-cost target is yours. Data is stored in this browser only: use Backup JSON or Export CSV regularly.
