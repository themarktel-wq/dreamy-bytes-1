# Price Hike Reality Check

A single HTML file that shows restaurant and cafe owners how much volume they can lose before a menu price increase hurts profit. Enter the price, unit cost, weekly units and the proposed increase and it gives the break-even volume loss (how many units a week you can lose and still make the same profit), plus a scenario table for the volume drop you think is realistic. Switch to whole-menu mode to test an increase across many items at once, weighted by units, with per-item overrides, and print a one-page summary. Open it in any browser; no install, no account, nothing is uploaded.

**Features**
- One item: margin now and after, break-even volume loss %, units you can lose per week
- Editable volume-drop scenarios: profit per week and change vs today
- Whole menu: per-item increase overrides, weighted whole-menu break-even, profit at your tested volume drop
- Printable one-page summary, copy as text, JSON export/import
- Saved in your browser; works on phone and desktop

**Who it is for:** independent restaurant, cafe and food-truck owners and managers deciding on a menu price increase.

**Important:** this tool does maths only. It does not predict how customers will react and makes no claim about price elasticity; you enter the volume drop you expect. Industry context: the James Beard Foundation and Deloitte 2026 independent restaurant report found that operators raising prices more than 10% were most likely to expect lower profits. That is survey data about expectations, not proof that price increases cause lower profit. Not financial advice.
