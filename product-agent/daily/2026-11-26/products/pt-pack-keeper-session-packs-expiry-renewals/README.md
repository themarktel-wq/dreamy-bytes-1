# PT Pack Keeper: Session Packs, Expiry and Renewals

A single-file tracker for personal trainers who sell prepaid session packs. It keeps each client's pack balance and expiry date, applies your own late-cancel and no-show rule, and gives you a weekly "who needs attention" list: low on sessions, out of sessions, expiring soon, expired with sessions left, or gone quiet, each with a ready renewal or check-in message. Open `index.html` in any browser. No install, no account, no client cap, no monthly fee, works offline.

## What you get
- Clients with pack size, price, start date and expiry rule (days).
- Session log: done, cancelled by the client (with hours of notice) or no-show. Your rules decide whether a late cancel or a no-show uses a session.
- Dashboard "This week": low balance, out of sessions, expiring soon, expired, ghost risk, with copyable messages (low balance, renewal, expiring, expired, check-in).
- Renew a pack in two clicks; previous packs are kept in the client's history.
- Rules you set: notice hours, whether late cancels / no-shows use a session, low-balance level, expiring window, ghost-risk days.
- CSV export, JSON backup and restore. Data stays in your browser.
- Fictional sample clients so you can see it working; remove them with one click.

## Who it is for
Solo personal trainers who chase renewals, lose clients who quietly stop turning up, and do not want a client-capped subscription.

## Good to know
- Ghost risk is a simple rule of thumb (days since the last completed session). It is not a prediction and no retention rate is claimed.
- All thresholds and sample data are examples. Check local rules and your client agreements before charging late-cancel fees or expiring unused sessions.
- Tested in Chromium at desktop and 375 px phone width; not tested in Safari or Firefox. Use Backup JSON or Export CSV regularly.
- It does not send messages or take payments; you copy the messages and send them yourself.
