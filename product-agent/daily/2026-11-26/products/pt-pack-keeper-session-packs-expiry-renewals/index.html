Your rules

All thresholds are your own settings. The ghost-risk flag is a simple rule of thumb (days since the last session), not a prediction and not based on any retention statistic. Check local rules before charging late-cancel fees or letting sessions expire.

How PT Pack Keeper works

Balance = sessions in the pack - sessions used since the pack start date. A session is used when it is done, when a cancellation comes with less notice than your rule (if you set late cancels to use a session), or on a no-show (if you set no-shows to use a session).

Expiry = pack start + expiry days. Ghost risk = the client still has sessions but it has been more than your set number of days since their last completed session (or since the pack start if none).

This week lists clients who are low on sessions, out of sessions, expiring soon, expired with sessions left, or at ghost risk, each with a ready renewal or check-in message you can copy.

Renew starts a new pack for the client from a date you choose; old sessions stay in the log but no longer count against the new pack.
