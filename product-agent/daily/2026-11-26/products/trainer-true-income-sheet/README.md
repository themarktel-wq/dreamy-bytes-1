# Trainer True Income Sheet

A spreadsheet for solo personal trainers that shows what you really earn per hour once late cancellations, prep, travel, admin hours, gym or rent fees, software, per-client seat fees and payment fees are counted. Enter your own numbers and the sheet gives your true hourly income and net income per year, the price per session and pack price needed to reach your income target, and a client-scenarios table showing how income and weekly hours change as you add clients, flagging weeks above the hours limit you set.

**What you get**
- Calculator tab: 18 yellow inputs, year summary, true hourly income, hours-limit check
- Price per session, pack price and list price (with your pack discount) needed for your target
- Client Scenarios tab: 4 to 30 clients, with per-client software fees included
- Start Here guide; works in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice; no macros

**Who it is for:** solo personal trainers and small-studio coaches who want to price sessions and packs from real numbers.

**Notes:** example numbers are placeholders, not benchmarks. Software, seat and payment fees are entered by you: no platform price is assumed. Not tax, legal or financial advice.
