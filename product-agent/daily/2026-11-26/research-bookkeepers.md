## Gaps (honest)
- No forum threads, no Meta Ad Library, no small-practice-only survey on late receipts specifically. Many sources are practice-software vendors. Gate: sources 5; quantitative B1-B4; gap: practice tools cost $19-89+ per user per month with per-seat pricing; a paid chase tracker/automation without seats seems unaddressed (not exhaustively verified). Competitor prices >=5: Financial Cents tiers, TaxDome, Karbon, Canopy.
- Tax/accounting advice is out of scope: products are workflow tools and calculators only.
## Personas
1. **Freelance bookkeeper (5-25 clients):** chases receipts/statements every month; trigger: month-end deadline with missing documents.
2. **2-5 person small practice:** wants Karbon-like workflow without per-seat fees.
