# Billable Hours and Overhead Sheet

A spreadsheet for plumbing, HVAC and other field-service shop owners who do not know their overhead per billable hour. List your yearly overhead, enter each technician's scheduled and billable hours and wages, and see overhead per billable hour, labor cost per billable hour, total cost per billable hour, the minimum hourly price for your target margin, and how many jobs per day you need to break even at your average ticket.

## What is inside
- **Start Here** - short instructions.
- **Inputs** - payroll burden %, average ticket, parts cost % of the ticket, working days per year, target margin.
- **Overhead** - up to 20 overhead items (rent, insurance, trucks, software, admin, marketing...), each entered as monthly or annual; the sheet converts to a yearly cost.
- **Technicians** - up to 8 people: annual wages, scheduled and billable hours per week, weeks worked. It shows hours per year, billable % of scheduled, loaded labor cost and labor cost per billable hour.
- **Results** - totals, overhead and labor cost per billable hour, minimum price per hour, break-even jobs per year, per working day and per technician per day, break-even revenue, and a what-if table for billable hours from -20% to +30%.
- Sample shop with fictional numbers so you can see it working; replace the yellow cells with your own.

## Who it is for
Solo and small-shop plumbing, HVAC, electrical and similar owners who price by feel and want to know what an hour really costs.

## Good to know
- Billable hours are hours you can actually invoice, not hours you pay for. Enter your own real figures; many shops bill far fewer hours than they schedule.
- Overhead per billable hour = total yearly overhead / total yearly billable hours. Example: $120,000 / 1,200 hours = $100.
- Works in Excel 2016 or later, Excel for Mac, Excel Online, Google Sheets (upload, then Open with Google Sheets) and LibreOffice. No macros.
- All starting numbers are fictional examples, not industry averages. Planning tool only: not tax, accounting or financial advice.
