# Chair Math: Commission vs Booth Rent Calculator

A single-file calculator that compares commission, booth rent and hybrid (lower rent plus commission) chair deals using the stylist's or salon owner's own numbers. See net income per week, month and year under each deal, the weekly revenue where the deals cross, and a printable side-by-side. Open `index.html` in any browser. No install, no account, works offline; your inputs stay in this browser.

## What you get
- Inputs: weekly service revenue, product cost % of services, weekly retail sales and retail margin, weeks worked, plus the terms of each deal (commission %, booth rent, hybrid rent and %, retail profit shares, who pays product cost) and the salon's cost of providing the chair.
- Stylist view and salon owner view of all three deals, with the highest highlighted.
- Break-even revenue for every pair of deals, and which pays more above that revenue.
- "What if your revenue changes?" table from half to double your revenue with the best deal highlighted.
- Printable side-by-side comparison. Built-in maths check: stylist net + owner net always equals revenue + retail profit - product cost - chair cost.

## How it works
For each deal the weekly result is a straight line in service revenue R: net = slope x R + intercept. The break-even revenue between two deals is the R where the lines cross.

## Who it is for
Independent stylists, barbers and small salon owners deciding between commission and booth rent (or negotiating a hybrid) who want the maths instead of vendor blog ranges.

## Good to know
- The starting numbers are fictional examples. No market percentage or "typical" split is assumed: enter the offers you actually have.
- It is arithmetic, not advice on what a fair deal is. It ignores taxes, tips, insurance and the employment-law differences between employees and booth renters (rules vary by country and state), and slow or fully booked weeks.
- Tested in Chromium at desktop and 375 px phone width; not tested in Safari or Firefox.
