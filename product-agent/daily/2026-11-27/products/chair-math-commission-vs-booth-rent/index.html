How Chair Math works

For every deal the weekly result is a straight line in your service revenue R: net = slope x R + intercept.

Commission: stylist net = R x commission % + retail share - (R x product % if the stylist pays product). Booth rent: stylist net = R - rent + retail share - (R x product % if the stylist pays product). Hybrid: stylist net = R x commission % - rent + retail share - product cost if the stylist pays.

Retail share = weekly retail sales x retail margin % x your share %. The salon owner gets the rest of the retail profit.

Break-even revenue between two deals is the revenue where the two lines cross: R = (intercept2 - intercept1) / (slope1 - slope2). Above or below it, the other deal pays the stylist more.

Salon owner net = what the salon receives (its share of services, rent, its share of retail profit) minus product it pays for and the cost of providing the chair. Stylist net + owner net always equals revenue + retail profit - product cost - chair cost: a built-in check on the maths.

Honest notes. This is arithmetic on the numbers you type, not advice on what a fair deal is. It ignores taxes, tips, insurance, employment-law differences between employees and renters (rules vary by country and state), and slow or fully booked weeks. Your inputs are saved in this browser only.
