# Estimate Chaser: Unsold Estimate Follow-Up Tracker

A single HTML file for service-business owners whose estimates die in a text thread. Log every estimate with customer, job, amount and the date you sent it, and the tracker tells you who to follow up with today, on the schedule you choose (default day 2, 7 and 14), with a short message ready to copy. See your open pipeline value, how old your open estimates are, which big estimates are most at risk, and your win rate by month with the reasons you lose jobs. Open it in any browser; no install, no account, nothing is uploaded.

**Features**
- Estimate list: customer, contact, job, amount, date sent, status (open / won / lost), reason lost
- Follow-up cadence you can edit; "Follow up today" list sorted by most overdue, with a message template and copy button
- Pipeline: open count and value, aging buckets, biggest open estimates
- Win / loss: win rate (won / (won + lost)) by month, won and lost value, lost reasons
- CSV export, JSON backup/restore, saved in your browser; works on phone and desktop

**Who it is for:** small trade and service shops (about 3 to 8 technicians) such as HVAC, plumbing and electrical, where estimates are sent by text or email and rarely chased.

**Important:** the tool keeps your list and reminders. It does not promise that follow-up closes any particular share of estimates; claims you may see online about this come from vendors and are unverified. Starting entries are examples.
