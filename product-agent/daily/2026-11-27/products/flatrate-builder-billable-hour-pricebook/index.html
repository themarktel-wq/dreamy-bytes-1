How FlatRate Builder works

Loaded labor rate = (annual wages x (1 + burden %) + vehicle cost) / billable hours per technician.

Overhead per billable hour = total yearly overhead / (billable hours per technician x number of technicians).

Cost of an hour = loaded rate + overhead per hour. Example: $120,000 of labor cost / 1,200 billable hours = $100 per hour before overhead.

Job cost = hours x cost of an hour + parts at cost x (1 + markup for that line). Flat price = job cost / (1 - target margin), rounded up if you set rounding. The margin shown is measured on labor plus marked-up parts at the final (rounded) price; the editor also shows profit over labor and parts at cost.

Good / better / best: each job has up to three options with their own hours and parts; the customer picks one.

Honest notes. Example inputs follow publicly described flat-rate methods (vendor guides) and are not an industry standard or advice on what to charge.
