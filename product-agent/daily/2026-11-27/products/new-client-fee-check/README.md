# New-Client Fee Check: Marketplace Fee Calculator

A single-file calculator for independent stylists and barbers that shows whether a booking marketplace's new-client fee pays off. Enter your average ticket, the share you keep after supplies and chair costs, and your rebooking numbers; compare several platforms side by side on the real cost per new client, the value of that client over 12 months, the monthly net and how many repeat visits you need to cover the cost.

## What you get
- Your inputs: average ticket, share of ticket you keep, new-client rebook rate, extra visits from a client who rebooks, and the % of marketplace clients who would have booked you anyway
- Editable platform table (monthly price, new-client fee %, minimum fee, new clients per month the platform brings you). Presets are labelled "as reported" because they come from competitor-written comparison sites and may be out of date
- Per platform: first-visit fee, all-in cost per new client (fee plus subscription spread over the clients it brings), 12-month value per new client, net per client, monthly net, fee as % of value, visits needed to cover the cost, and the rebook rate needed
- The row with the highest monthly net is highlighted; negative numbers in red
- Printable summary; saves in your browser; works offline; phone friendly

## How it works
Expected visits in 12 months = 1 + rebook rate x extra visits. Value of a new client = ticket x share you keep x expected visits. The fee is charged on the first visit: max(minimum fee, fee % x ticket). Monthly net = genuinely new clients x value - fees on all clients the platform brings - monthly price. Set "share you keep" to 100 to compare on revenue instead of margin.

## Good to know
Fee terms change often and differ by country, plan and promotion. Check each platform's current terms and edit the table. The tool is arithmetic: it ignores tips, retail, whether a marketplace would really send you those clients, and your time. The starting numbers are examples, not typical results.

## Who it is for
Independent stylists, barbers and booth renters deciding whether to accept marketplace new clients and which platform to use.
