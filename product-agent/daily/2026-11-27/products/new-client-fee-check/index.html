New-Client Fee Check

Does a marketplace's new-client fee pay off for you? Compare platforms using your own ticket, margin and rebooking numbers. Runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

Platform fees below are editable presets, as reported by competitor-written comparison sites, and may be out of date. Check each platform's current terms before deciding. Your own numbers matter more than the presets.

1. Your numbers

Expected visits in 12 months = 1 + rebook rate x extra visits. Value of a new client = ticket x share you keep x expected visits. Set "share you keep" to 100 to compare on revenue instead of margin. The fee is charged on the first visit only.

Set "new clients per month" to how many new clients that platform really brings you (marketplaces bring clients you pay for; a subscription-only or direct-booking setup brings only the clients your own marketing finds, so enter 0 to see its price alone). Monthly net is the extra monthly value of the clients each platform brings, after its fees.

All-in cost = first-visit fee + monthly price / new clients per month (shown as the fee alone when it brings no clients). Monthly net = genuinely new clients x value - fees on all clients it brings - monthly price. "Highest monthly net" is a simple arithmetic ranking, not advice: it ignores whether the marketplace would really send you those clients, tips, retail, and your time.
