# Planner Package and Hourly Rate Sheet

A spreadsheet for independent wedding and event planners that shows the real hourly rate of each package. Enter the hours you spend in each phase (design and planning, vendor management, day-of, admin, travel), the price, direct cost per event and how many of each package you plan to sell, and the sheet gives the true hourly rate after fees and software, flags packages below your target, shows the price needed to reach your target hourly rate, and totals your yearly hours, income and capacity use so you can see whether your package mix is realistic.

**What you get**
- Settings tab: target hourly rate, fee %, software, other costs, yearly hours, yearly income target; annual summary (revenue, fees, direct costs, net income, hours, share of capacity, net per hour, hours check)
- Packages tab: up to 8 packages with hours by phase, true hourly rate, price needed, "Below target" flag, contribution per year and events needed alone for your yearly target
- Start Here guide; works in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice; no macros

**Who it is for:** independent wedding, event and day-of coordinators who want to price packages from real hours.

**Notes:** all prices, hours and fees are yours; the example numbers are placeholders. For context only, The Knot's 2026 Real Weddings Study (a secondary report) cites an average US planner cost of about $2,100 for 2024 weddings; the sheet does not use it. Not tax, legal or financial advice.
