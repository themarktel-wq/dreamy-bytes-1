# Rebook Radar: Rebooking and Retention Tracker

A single HTML file for salon owners and barbers who want clients to come back. Add each client with their usual stylist, usual interval in weeks and last visit, then log visits and whether the client rebooked at checkout. Rebook Radar shows your rebooking percentage per stylist and month against a target you set yourself, ranks clients who are overdue for their usual interval (with grace days you choose) and lists who is due soon, each with a short message ready to copy. Open it in any browser; no install, no account, nothing is uploaded.

**Features**
- Clients with usual stylist, interval (weeks) and last visit; expected-back date and status
- Visit log with "rebooked at checkout" yes/no; logging a visit updates the last visit date
- Rebook % per stylist and month, compared with your own target
- Overdue list (past expected date plus grace days) and due-soon list, with copy-ready messages
- CSV export of visits, JSON backup/restore, saved in your browser; works on phone and desktop

**Who it is for:** small salon owners, stylists and barbers who track rebooking and want a simple win-back list.

**Notes:** all percentages come from your own log. Industry figures shown inside the tool are vendor references only (a 2025 Zenoti benchmark piece describes top performers as above about 65% rebooking; another Zenoti piece uses 60% as a target and 55% as a floor) and their definitions differ: set your own target. Starting entries are examples.
