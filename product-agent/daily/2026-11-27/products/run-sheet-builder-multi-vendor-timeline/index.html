How Run Sheet Builder works

1. In "Event & locations" enter the event, each location and how many vendors can work there at once (capacity), your key moments (ceremony, dinner) and emergency contacts.

2. Add each vendor with their role, contact, location, arrival time, setup minutes, the time they must be ready by (optional) and load-in notes.

3. A vendor occupies their location from arrival until arrival + setup. A conflict is any time when more vendors are in a location than its capacity. A tight buffer is when the next vendor arrives less than your minimum buffer after the previous one finishes. Other warnings: setup ends after the vendor's ready-by time, arrival outside the venue's load-in window, or a vendor with no arrival time.

4. "Run sheets" prints a one-page sheet per vendor with their times, who else is in the same location, key moments and emergency contacts.

Honest notes. The checks only know what you enter (travel time, traffic, elevators and venue rules are not modelled). Sample event data is fictional. Data is stored in this browser only: use Export JSON to back up.
