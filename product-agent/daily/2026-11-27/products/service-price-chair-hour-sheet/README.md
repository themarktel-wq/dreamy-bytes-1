# Service Price and Chair-Hour Sheet

A spreadsheet for independent stylists, barbers and small salon owners that prices every service from the time it takes, the product it uses and what each chair hour must earn after rent and overhead. Enter your target income per chair hour, rent and overhead, chair hours available and the share you have booked, then list your services. The sheet shows profit per chair hour for each service (also on a heavy product-use day, which matters for colour), flags services that fall below your target, suggests the price that reaches it, and shows what your monthly service mix earns.

**What you get**
- Settings tab: target per chair hour, rent, overhead, hours, booked share, fee %; overhead per booked hour; monthly mix results (revenue, product, fees, net, net per chair hour)
- Services tab: up to 20 services with typical and heavy-use product cost, profit per chair hour, "Below target" flag, suggested price, price gap
- Start Here guide; works in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice; no macros

**Who it is for:** independent stylists, barbers and salon owners who want to know which services really pay for the chair.

**Notes:** example numbers are placeholders, not market prices or benchmarks. Fees are entered by you: no commission is assumed. Not tax, legal or financial advice.
