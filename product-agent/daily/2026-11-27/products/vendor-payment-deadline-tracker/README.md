# Vendor Payment and Deadline Tracker

An Excel / Google Sheets workbook for independent event planners and day-of coordinators. List every vendor payment and every contract deadline across your events and see at a glance what is overdue, what is due this week and how much is still outstanding.

## What you get
- Events sheet (up to 10): event date, contracted, paid, outstanding, overdue amount and days to the event, calculated per event
- Payments sheet (up to 60): event, vendor, item (deposit, balance, installment, other), amount, due date, paid so far, paid date; outstanding amount, days until due and status (Paid / Overdue / Due soon / Upcoming) calculated, colour-coded
- Deadlines sheet (up to 40): menu selection, final guest count, timeline, contract signing, floor plan, tech rider or other; Done? drop-down; status calculated
- "This Week" sheet: overdue and due-soon payments and deadlines, soonest first (up to 15 of each)
- Summary: total contracted, paid, outstanding, overdue and due-soon amounts, counts of overdue, due-soon and done items, and a chart of outstanding by event
- Settings: today's date (or =TODAY()) and how many days ahead counts as "due soon"
- Works in Excel 2016+, Excel Online, Google Sheets and LibreOffice; no macros; any currency

## Good to know
The sample events, vendors, amounts and dates are made-up examples; delete the sample rows before using your own data. The tracker records what you enter; it does not contact vendors or move money, and it does not read your contracts, so check them for the real terms and any late fees. Not legal, tax or financial advice.

## Who it is for
Independent wedding and event planners and day-of coordinators who keep vendor payment dates and contract deadlines in emails and scattered notes.
