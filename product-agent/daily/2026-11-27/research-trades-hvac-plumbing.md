## Gaps (honest)
- No Reddit/forum threads, no Meta Ad Library, no independent survey on missed calls or estimates (all figures vendor-sourced), no flat-rate complaint data. PHCC/NATE primary data not read.
- Gate: sources 5+; quantitative H1, H2, H3; gap: per-user pricing and quote-only vendors (H2); small shops compute overhead by gut (claim from guides, not verified as a survey result). Competitor prices >=5: Jobber, Housecall Pro, Workiz, ServiceTitan, plus tiers.
- Overlap check: QuoteSweep (cleaning) used a cost/(1-margin) method; trades products are different (billable-hour overhead, parts markup, pricebook).

2. **Small shop owner (3-8 techs):** unsold estimates and missed calls; trigger: slow month.
