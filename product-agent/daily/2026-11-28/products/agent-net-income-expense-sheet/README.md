# Agent Net Income and Expense Sheet

A spreadsheet that turns real estate closings into what you actually keep. Enter each closing with price, commission %, any referral fee, your brokerage split and transaction fee, then log business expenses. It shows gross commission, what referrals and the brokerage take, net per deal, monthly and yearly net before tax, an estimated tax set-aside at the percentage you choose, and what is left.

## What is inside
- **Start Here** - short instructions and formulas.
- **Settings** - your usual split (the share you keep), usual transaction fee, tax set-aside %, the year shown, and up to 12 expense categories.
- **Transactions** - up to 100 closings: date, property, side, sale price, commission %, referral fee %, split % and transaction fee (leave blank to use Settings), other deal costs. Auto columns: gross commission, referral fee, brokerage share, your commission, net per deal.
- **Expenses** - up to 200 business expenses with a category dropdown.
- **Summary** - yearly KPIs, a month-by-month table (closings, volume, gross, referral + brokerage, fees, net from deals, expenses, net before tax, set-aside, left), expenses by category, a chart, and a context box.
- Eight fictional sample closings and fifteen sample expenses so you can see it working; replace them with your own.

## Who it is for
Solo and part-time real estate agents who see gross commission but not what is left after splits, fees and expenses.

## Good to know
- The context box quotes public NAR figures (typical agent 9 transaction sides, median gross income $59,200; median business expenses $8,010) as context only. They are not a target, a forecast or a comparison for your business.
- The tax set-aside is simply your percentage applied to net before tax (never below zero). This workbook gives no tax advice: ask a tax professional what to set aside.
- Works in Excel 2016 or later, Excel for Mac, Excel Online, Google Sheets (upload, then Open with Google Sheets) and LibreOffice. No macros.
- Not tax, legal or accounting advice. All sample numbers are fictional.
