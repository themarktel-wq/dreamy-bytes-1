# Brief: Estimate vs Actual Variance Tracker (d20261128-09)
- Niche: movers. Persona: small mover.
- Concept: feedback loop for hourly quotes: variance per job and by type, crew, stairs and distance band, tolerance flags and suggested adjustment factors with a minimum-sample guard.
