# Brief: Instant Inquiry Reply n8n Flow (d20261128-07)
- Niche: real estate agents. Persona: solo real estate agent.
- Concept: n8n flow for instant first reply, lead logging with spam and repeat handling, agent alert and next-morning uncontacted list. CRMs (Follow Up Boss, Wise Agent, kvCORE) charge per user per month; this is a one-time flow.
- Evidence: A3 (Pied Piper 2026 study of 2,198 inquiries to 21 large brands, many unanswered or slow, via Florida Realtors coverage; plus trade claims that a missed lead costs a commission; Med-Low). Do not quote vendor claims as facts in marketing.
- Scope notes: webhook trigger only (an email-parsing trigger was not built because it cannot be tested here); SMS alert included but disabled by default; no AI, deterministic rules.
- Compliance: README warns that email consent rules vary and that unsubscribe requests must be honoured by the agent; no legal advice.
