# Lead Clock: Inquiry Response Timer and Callback Queue

A single-file tool for solo real estate agents who miss calls and messages while at showings. Log every inquiry with a timestamp, see how many minutes each lead has been waiting, work a ranked callback queue, and reply fast with ready-to-send scripts. Open `index.html` in any browser. No install, no account, no subscription, works offline.

## What you get
- Inquiry log: source (phone, web form, portal, open house, referral...), name, phone, time received (defaults to now), note.
- Callback queue of every uncontacted lead with a live "waiting" timer, coloured green / amber / red by limits you set. Order: red first, then your source priority, then the longest wait. One tap on "I contacted them" stops the clock and records the response time.
- Reply script library (missed call, web form inquiry, at a showing, no-answer follow-up, voicemail) with the lead's first name and your name filled in; edit the wording.
- Weekly stats for the last 7 days: inquiries, median response time, how many were answered within your green limit, how many are still uncontacted, and a per-source table.
- CSV export, JSON backup and restore. Data stays in your browser.
- Six fictional sample leads so you can see it working; remove them with one click.

## Who it is for
Solo and small-team agents whose leads wait while they are at showings, in meetings or off the phone.

## Good to know
- The clock counts every minute, including nights and weekends.
- Public studies of real estate inquiries (for example a 2026 Pied Piper study of inquiries to large brands) found many were answered slowly or not at all. This tool makes no claim about how much faster replies change your results or conversions.
- Colour limits, source priorities and script wording are yours to set.
- Tested in Chromium at desktop and 375 px phone width; not tested in Safari or Firefox. Use Backup JSON or Export CSV regularly.
- It does not call, text or connect to any CRM or portal: you log inquiries and copy the scripts yourself.
