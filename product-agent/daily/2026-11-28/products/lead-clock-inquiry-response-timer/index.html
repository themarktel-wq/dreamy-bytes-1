How Lead Clock works

1. Log each inquiry as it arrives (phone, web form, portal, open house...). Time received defaults to now.

2. "Callback queue" lists every lead not yet contacted, with minutes waiting, coloured green / amber / red by the limits you set. Order: red first, then your source priority, then the longest wait. Press "I contacted them" to stop the clock.

3. "Reply scripts" gives short messages for missed calls and showings: copy, paste, send.

4. "Weekly stats" shows your median response time, how many leads were contacted within your green limit and how many are still uncontacted.

Honest notes. The clock counts every minute, including nights and weekends. Public studies of real estate inquiries found many were answered slowly or not at all, but this tool makes no claim about how much faster replies change your results. Sample leads are fictional. Data is stored in this browser only: use Backup JSON or Export CSV regularly.
