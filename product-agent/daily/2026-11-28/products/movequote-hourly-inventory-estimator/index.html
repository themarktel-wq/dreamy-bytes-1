How MoveQuote works

Hourly total = billable hours x crew x rate per mover + truck fee + extras. Billable hours = the larger of your minimum hours and (working hours + billed travel hours).

Inventory mode converts your item table into working hours: hours = total cubic feet / (cubic feet per mover-hour x crew). Then the same hourly formula applies. The item volumes and the productivity figure are your own numbers.

Range = likely total x (1 - low %) to likely total x (1 + high %), with the percentages you set.

Estimate type (non-binding, binding, not-to-exceed) selects the wording block printed on the quote. Replace the placeholder wording with text that fits the rules where you operate.

Honest notes. Starting rates, items and wording are fictional examples. Estimate rules vary by jurisdiction and interstate moves have federal rules that this tool does not cover. Estimates can be wrong: the range is only as good as the numbers you enter. Data is stored in this browser only: use Export JSON to back up.
