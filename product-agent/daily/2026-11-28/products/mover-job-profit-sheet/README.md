# Mover Job Profit Sheet

An Excel / Google Sheets workbook for owner-operator and small local movers that shows what each job really earns after truck, crew, fuel, a claims reserve and card fees. Enter your cost rates once, enter each job, and see profit, margin, profit per hour and the longest the job could run before it loses money. A quote helper gives you the minimum price for your target margin.

## What you get
- Settings: truck cost per hour, fuel cost per mile, crew wage and payroll burden %, claims reserve % (your own estimate), card fee % and fixed fee, overhead per job, target margin and summary year
- Jobs sheet (up to 60 jobs): date, reference, crew size, hours, miles, price and other direct cost; crew, truck, fuel, claims reserve, payment fees, overhead, total cost, profit, margin, profit per hour, break-even hours and a status (OK / Below target / Loss) with colours
- Quote Helper: enter crew, hours, miles and other cost for a planned job and get the minimum price to break even, the minimum price for your target margin and the hourly rate it implies
- Monthly Summary for the year you choose: jobs, revenue, cost, profit, margin, hours, profit per hour and average profit per job, with a chart
- Sources and Notes sheet; works in Excel 2016+, Excel Online, Google Sheets and LibreOffice; no macros; any currency

## Good to know
Every rate and sample job is an example; use your real truck, wage and fuel numbers. The claims reserve is your own estimate of what you set aside for damage claims, not an industry figure. Not included: income tax, long-distance weight or cubic-foot pricing, storage, tips. Add unpaid travel time to hours if you pay for it. A planning tool, not tax, legal or financial advice.

## Who it is for
Owner-operator and small local movers who price by the hour and want to know their margin per job before setting minimums.
