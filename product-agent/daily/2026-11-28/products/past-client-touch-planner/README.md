# Past-Client Touch Planner

A single HTML file that gives solo and part-time real estate agents a weekly "who to contact" list for past clients and their sphere. Add each contact with closing date, birthday and a tier (A, B or C), set how many touches a year each tier gets, and the planner works out who is overdue or due in the next 7 days, plus birthdays and home anniversaries in the next 14 days, each with a short editable message prompt. Log every call, note, email or visit and the clock resets. Import your contacts from a CSV and export them back out. Open it in any browser; no install, no account, nothing is uploaded.

**Features**
- Contacts with closing date, birthday, tier and contact details; CSV import and export
- Cadence per tier you set (touches per year; days between touches = 365 / touches)
- Weekly due list with overdue flags, a touch log (type and note), and last / next touch dates
- Birthday and home-anniversary reminders (Feb 29 handled), with editable message prompts
- JSON backup and restore, saved in your browser; works on phone and desktop

**Who it is for:** solo and part-time agents who want a simple, consistent past-client follow-up routine without paying for a CRM.

**Important:** this is an admin and planning tool only. It does not send messages and makes no claim about results. Follow your local marketing, privacy and consent rules (for example do-not-call and email/text consent). Starting entries are examples.
