Past-Client Touch Planner

A weekly "who to contact" list for your past clients and sphere. Works offline; nothing is uploaded.

Starting entries are examples. This is a planning and record tool only. Follow your local marketing, privacy and consent rules (for example do-not-call and email/text consent) when you contact people. It makes no claim about results.
