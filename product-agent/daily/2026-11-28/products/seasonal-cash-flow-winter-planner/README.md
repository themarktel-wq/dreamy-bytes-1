# Seasonal Cash Flow and Winter Planner

A 12-month cash-flow spreadsheet for seasonal lawn-care, landscaping and outdoor service businesses. Enter your own revenue curve by service, variable and fixed costs and the cash you start the year with, and the sheet shows net cash and closing cash for every month, your lowest cash point and which month it falls in, and the reserve you need to carry through the slow months. Switch winter add-ons (leaf removal, holiday lights, gutter cleaning) and snow contracts on and off to compare scenarios, and use the snow capacity check to see whether your trucks and hours can serve the contracts you sell.

**What you get**
- Setup tab: starting cash, safety buffer, variable cost %, fixed monthly costs, snow-contract inputs and the snow capacity check
- Revenue tab: 8 core services by month, 5 winter add-ons with Include Yes/No and direct cost %, snow events per month
- Cash Flow tab: monthly revenue, costs, net cash flow, opening and closing cash, lowest cash point, slow-month deficit, reserve to carry, contribution of add-ons and snow
- Start Here guide; works in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice; no macros

**Who it is for:** small lawn, landscape and outdoor-service crews that earn most of their revenue in a few months and want to plan the slow ones.

**Notes:** example numbers are for a small crew and are placeholders, not benchmarks. The reserve is arithmetic on your inputs, not a forecast. Snow work needs equipment, insurance and reliable storms: size contracts to the hours you really have. Not tax, legal or financial advice.
