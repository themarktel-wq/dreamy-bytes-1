# Research: website builders to sell in the US (Nov 2026)

Sources are vendor and comparison blogs; treat figures as directional. No source gave verified US sales rankings by niche.

## What the search showed
- General website-builder market is large and growing; one vendor-published report (BaseKit, a white-label vendor) says it will roughly double to over $6B by 2030. Elementor reports about a quarter of owners want AI-assisted design. Wix, Squarespace, Shopify and Hostinger dominate reviews (mostly affiliate roundups).
- Home-service contractors: Housecall Pro, citing Blue Corona, says about 63% of consumers find service professionals through a website; WebFX/Cube Creative data cited by PipelineOn says about 70% prefer to book online, yet many contractor sites only route to an inbox. Guides recommend click-to-call, quote forms, service-area pages, reviews and fast mobile pages. Framer sells one-page templates for this niche. (Figures are unverified vendor claims.)
- Restaurants: QR-menu tools run from free to about $100 a month (Menulook $5, QR Menu Generator $9.99, Clickymenu $12, MenuTiger/MyQRMenu/QR Menu Supreme about $19, UpMenu website builder about $89-$119; pages mix currencies).
- Link-in-bio: Beacons Creator Plus about $30/mo, Stan about $29/mo, Payhip Plus $29/mo; cheaper tools from about $7-$10/mo. No lifetime-deal pricing was found.

## Why these five
1. **LinkPage Studio**: low-friction impulse buy; every creator and small shop needs one; competitors charge monthly, a one-time price is a clear angle.
2. **LocalPro**: highest willingness to pay; sells to contractors directly or to freelancers/agencies who build sites for them (the Lifetime licence allows client work).
3. **MenuMaker**: restaurants pay monthly for menu/QR tools; a one-time site + menu + QR is a simple alternative.
4. **PortfolioPro**: image-heavy, appeals to photographers, designers and creatives who want a site they own.
5. **LaunchPad**: landing pages for digital products, courses, apps and agencies; sells to the same audience that buys templates.

## Selling
- Position as "own your site files, no monthly fee, host anywhere free".
- Target freelancers and small agencies with the white-label licence: they can build many client sites with one purchase.
- Channels: Gumroad / Lemon Squeezy, Etsy (digital downloads), AppSumo-style lifetime-deal sites, Product Hunt, Facebook groups for contractors, restaurateurs and freelancers.
- Bundle idea: all five for $129-$149 (single prices total $215).
- Add a paid service: "I build it for you" using the builder (setup fee) to raise average order value.
