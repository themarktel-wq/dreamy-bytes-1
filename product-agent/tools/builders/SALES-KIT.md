# Sales kit: pain points, pitches and offers for the 5 website builders

Honest ranking first. Plain "website builder" is a crowded market (Wix, Squarespace and others), so never pitch these as "a website builder". Pitch each as the fix for one specific, costly problem, to one specific buyer.

| Rank | Product | Why it can sell | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | LocalPro ($59) | Sold to freelancers/agencies who build sites for local businesses: one licence, unlimited client sites, white-label | Needs you to reach those freelancers |
| 2 | MenuMaker ($39) | Restaurants already pay monthly for menu/QR tools; you offer site + menu + QR, one payment | Restaurant owners are busy and slow to reply |
| 3 | LaunchPad ($49) | Course/ebook/app creators need a landing page today, not a design project | Many free templates exist |
| 4 | PortfolioPro ($39) | Photographers and designers want a site they own | Buyers expect real photos and fancy animation |
| 5 | LinkPage ($29) | Impulse buy, "no monthly fee" angle | Linktree-style tools are free; weakest, use as a free lead magnet |

## The one pitch that works across all five
"Stop renting your website. Pay once, own the files, host anywhere for free."
Facts you can back (vendor pages, check before quoting): Beacons Creator Plus about $30/mo, Stan about $29/mo, Payhip Plus $29/mo, QR-menu tools about $5 to $119/mo. A one-time $29 to $59 licence pays for itself in about one to two months against those.

## 1. LocalPro (lead product)
**Buyer A (best): freelancers, small agencies, "I do websites on the side" people.**
- Pain: every local site takes days (layout, copy, mobile, SEO tags, contact form) so they cannot take more than a few clients.
- Promise: "Build a complete 4-page local business site in under 30 minutes, with the copy already written for plumbers, HVAC, roofers, cleaners, landscapers and electricians."
- Proof: 6 trade templates with full copy, quote form, click-to-call bar, FAQ, service areas, schema.org LocalBusiness, tested at phone width, exports a zip to any host.
- Money maths (example, check your own market): if a freelancer charges $500 per site, one site covers the $59 licence nearly ten times over. Say "example", not "guaranteed".
- Offer: $59 lifetime, white-label, unlimited client sites. Bonus: "first 20 buyers get the 6 trade templates' copy as a Google Doc".
- Headline options: "Build a plumber's website in 30 minutes. Charge for days." / "One licence. Unlimited client websites. No monthly fee."
- DM (Facebook groups, Reddit freelance communities, X): "I built a tool that makes a full 4-page site for local trades (plumbing, HVAC, roofing) in about 30 minutes: copy, quote form, SEO tags and click-to-call included. Pay once, build unlimited client sites, no branding. Want a free demo site for one of your clients to see the output?"
- Objection "Wix/WordPress already does this": "Those charge monthly per site. This exports plain files you host free anywhere, and the trade copy is already written, so you skip the blank-page problem."

**Buyer B: contractors themselves.**
- Pain (vendor-reported, unverified): most customers look for contractors online and many sites are not mobile-friendly or send enquiries to a lost inbox.
- Promise: "A mobile site with a quote form and a Call button, live today, no monthly fee."
- Approach: do not cold-pitch the software. Offer the finished site as a service ($300 to $800, your price), built with LocalPro in 30 minutes. Then upsell hosting/updates monthly.

## 2. MenuMaker
- Buyer: independent restaurants, cafes, food trucks, bakeries; and freelancers who serve them.
- Pain: PDF menus that are unreadable on a phone, no website, paying monthly for a QR menu, outdated prices.
- Promise: "A mobile menu site with a scannable QR code for your tables, live today, pay once."
- Proof: menu with categories, dietary tags, specials, hours, reservation button, QR that decodes to the menu page (verified with an independent QR reader), printable table card.
- Offer: $39 software, or done-for-you menu site $150 to $400 (your price) built in an hour.
- DM to a restaurant: "I noticed your menu is a PDF that is hard to read on a phone. I can turn it into a fast mobile menu page with a QR code for your tables, no monthly fee. I will make a free sample with your real menu if you send a photo of it."
- Objection "We already use a QR menu app": "How much per month? This is one payment and the files are yours."

## 3. LaunchPad
- Buyer: course creators, ebook/template sellers, app makers, webinar hosts, consultants.
- Pain: launch day is close, the landing page is not ready, designers are slow and expensive, builder subscriptions add up.
- Promise: "A landing page with pricing, FAQ, guarantee and countdown, ready the same day. Pay once."
- Proof: 6 launch templates (SaaS, course, ebook, app, webinar, agency), email capture, countdown timer, comparison table, pricing tiers, mobile tested.
- Headline: "Your offer deserves a page by tonight, not next month."
- Where: Product Hunt makers, course-creator groups, indie hacker communities.

## 4. PortfolioPro
- Buyer: photographers, designers, illustrators, interior designers, videographers, early-career creatives.
- Pain: social profiles cannot take bookings; Squarespace-type monthly fees; slow, bloated templates.
- Promise: "A fast portfolio you own, with a filterable gallery, packages and an enquiry form. No monthly fee."
- Be honest: it ships with generated art placeholders, buyers add their own photos. Sell the structure (pricing packages, enquiry flow, SEO), not stock imagery.
- Where: photography/design subreddits and groups, Etsy digital products.

## 5. LinkPage (use as the free hook)
- Pain: paying monthly for a page with a few links.
- Promise: "Your link-in-bio page, yours forever. $29 once."
- Best use: give the Free plan away. It collects email addresses and funnels creators to LaunchPad and PortfolioPro. Use the exported page's small "Made with LinkPage Studio" badge as a free ad (Free plan keeps it).

## Offer structure
- Good: single product ($29 to $59).
- Better: "Agency Bundle" all five for $129 to $149 (singles total $215).
- Best: done-for-you site using the tool, at your own price, with monthly updates/hosting retainer.
- Always give a free tier or a free sample site first. Every product has a working Free plan.

## 14-day plan
1. Days 1 to 2: put the 5 landing pages online (use LaunchPad itself to build them, a good demo), add your Gumroad/Lemon Squeezy links in `checkout.json`, replace the WhatsApp placeholder.
2. Days 3 to 5: record a 60-second screen video per product: pick a template, edit, export, open the site on a phone.
3. Days 6 to 10: post in 10 relevant communities (read each group's rules, no spam), 3 per day, always offering a free sample site.
4. Days 11 to 14: DM 20 restaurants and 20 contractors per day with a personalised sample (their real name and services). Track replies in a sheet.
5. Keep what works: usually the done-for-you service sells first, the software sells to freelancers after they see the demo.

## Do and do not
- Do show real sample output (the zips in `samples/`), screenshots on phone, and the 30-minute time claim only after you have timed yourself.
- Do not promise Google rankings, traffic or revenue. Do not use fake testimonials or invented statistics. Cold email must follow CAN-SPAM (real sender, address, opt-out).
- Do check the vendor price figures above before putting them in ads; they change.
