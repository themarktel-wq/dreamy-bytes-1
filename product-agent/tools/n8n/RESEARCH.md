# Research notes: trending US automations (Nov 2026)

Sources are vendor and community blogs; none is an independent survey. Treat as directional.

## Demand signals
- Lead follow-up and invoice/payment handling are named first automations across small-business guides ([DEV Community](https://dev.to/automatewithai/5-n8n-workflows-every-small-business-should-automate-in-2026-2c6e), [Automation Atlas](https://automationatlas.io/guides/automation-for-small-business/)).
- Highest buyer demand listed: lead qualification and follow-up, customer support resolution, voice reception and after-hours call handling, document-to-system processing, employee onboarding ([MindStudio](https://www.mindstudio.ai/blog/top-ai-automations-to-sell-2026/)).
- Agencies repeatedly cite client reporting, content/social repurposing, email triage and onboarding triggered by payment ([DEV Community](https://dev.to/n8nautomationcloud/5-ai-automation-workflows-every-agency-should-use-in-2026-26gk)).
- Guidance: automate the most repetitive tasks first, not the most complex.

## How the 50 map to demand
Sales 8, Marketing 13, Finance 8, Ecommerce 6, Support 5, Ops 5, HR 3, Local business 2 (see `CATALOG.md`). AI is used only where judgement is needed; everything else is rules (dedupe, aging, thresholds), which keeps cost near zero and results predictable.

## Pricing and selling
- Marketplace snapshot (Getly, Nov 2026): 28 n8n listings, median $39, middle half $21.50-$49. Guides cite $19-$79 per template and $500-$2,000 for niche kits.
- Suggested: $29-$49 per workflow, $149-$199 for a category bundle (for example the 8 Sales flows), higher when you add setup help.
- Channels: n8n Creator Hub (official exposure), Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy (fees differ by source; check current pages), Neura Market (creators keep ~90% per its own page). A community post reports template marketplaces can be quiet and that templates sell better with setup or support attached.
- Honest note: nothing here shows verified sales volumes. Expect to sell setup and support, not only files.
